# PocketSplit — Quick Start for the Shop Owner

A simple expense diary for your **shop** and your **home**.
It does not touch your sales software. It only remembers what you paid out.

---

## Open the app

Tap the green **PocketSplit** icon on your Home Screen. It works even when the internet is off.

---

## Record an expense in 5 seconds

1. Tap **Add expense**.
2. Type the **amount** (e.g. `2500`).
3. Type **what it was for** (e.g. `Electricity bill`).
4. Tap **Save**.

The date and time are filled in automatically.

- The **Shop / Home** buttons at the top choose which pocket the money came from.
- Paying for an older bill? Open **Date & time** and change it.
- Paid a worker? Open **Worker payment**, choose the worker, then choose
  **Salary**, **Advance**, **Additional payment** or **Repayment**.

---

## Shop pocket

Use it for anything the shop pays: salaries, advances, workers' lunch, electricity, gas,
rent, supplies, repairs, anything else.

**Workers** (Shop → Workers):
- Add each worker once by typing a name.
- Tap a worker to see everything paid to them.
- Money returned by a worker is recorded as a **Repayment**. It is *not* counted as spending.

---

## Home pocket

Use it for everything your household pays: groceries, chicken, beef, mutton, clothes, gas,
electricity, medicines, anything else.

Write any description you like. There is no fixed list.

---

## Check your spending

- **Overview** — this month's total, Shop and Home side by side, month arrows at the top.
- **Reports** — Today, Month or any custom dates. Shows Shop + Home, the biggest
  descriptions, and each worker's payments.

> PocketSplit shows **expenses only**. It never shows sales or profit.

---

## Find or fix an entry

1. Tap **Overview → See all**, or open any list.
2. Use the **search box** (type a word or a worker's name).
3. Change the date range with the **calendar button**.
4. Tap any entry to **edit** it. To remove it, tap the bin icon and confirm.
   Totals update immediately.

---

## Protect your records — do this every week

Your records are stored **inside this iPhone**. If the phone is lost or reset, they are gone
unless you have a backup file.

1. Tap **Backup**.
2. Tap **Save or share backup**.
3. Choose **Save to Files** (On My iPhone or iCloud Drive).
4. Also send the file to your computer every now and then (AirDrop, email, WhatsApp document).

To restore: **Backup → Choose backup file** → pick the file → **Merge** → confirm.

---

## Small tips

- **Possible duplicate** warning: if you save exactly the same expense twice, the app asks you
  to confirm. This stops accidental double entries.
- **Shop and Home never mix.** Their totals are always separate, and combined spending is always
  exactly Shop + Home.
- **Repayments are not spending.** They reduce what a worker owes you; they never inflate
  Shop totals.
- **Use only the app icon.** Data typed in a Safari browser tab stays in Safari and will not
  appear inside the app icon.
